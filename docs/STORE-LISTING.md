### Full description (max 4000)

```
You know the goal. You just don't know what to eat today.

MealPilot works out your daily calorie and macro targets from your own body data,
then builds a meal plan you can actually cook — from real ingredients you can buy
in an ordinary shop.

WHAT IT DOES

• Works out your daily budget from your weight, height, age and activity level,
  and tells you how fast you'll lose weight at that rate. You choose the pace; if
  the target would fall below your basal metabolic rate, it says so.
• Builds a 3, 7, 14 or 30-day meal plan with full nutrition figures.
• Writes the shopping list for you, grouped by aisle, with quantities added up.
• Reminds you when to eat what — and logs what you actually ate.
• Changes by conversation: "I won't have time to cook tomorrow", "rewrite the week
  to be cheaper", "swap Tuesday and Wednesday". It always asks before rewriting.

ALLERGIES AND EXCLUSIONS

The 14 EU allergens, gluten-, lactose- and casein-free, vegetarian and vegan diets,
and fructose, histamine and FODMAP sensitivity are all selectable. What you rule out
cannot appear in your plan — and the finished plan is machine-checked for it too.

This does not replace reading the label. With a serious allergy, always check what
is actually in the product.

ENGLISH AND HUNGARIAN

You pick a language on first launch and can switch it later in Settings. The language
does more than the interface: dish names, ingredients and the shopping list are all
written in it.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

No registration, no password, no email address. Your meal plan, your logs and your
body data live on your device. Planning sends your profile data to the planning
provider — your name and log history are not sent. You can delete everything with
one button, any time.

WHAT'S FREE

Logging, the shopping list, reminders and plans built from the app's own recipes stay
free. One plan and ten messages a month are included too.

The full plan comes with a subscription: unlimited planning and chat, 30-day plans,
and rewriting individual days.

MACHINE PLANNING

Meal plans are produced by a language model. That's fast and flexible, but it can be
wrong — so there's a report button on every plan and every dish. If something went
wrong, that's how it reaches us, and we fix it.

IMPORTANT

This app is for information only. It is not medical advice and is not suitable for
preventing, diagnosing or treating any illness. With an illness, in pregnancy, while
breastfeeding, with an eating disorder or on regular medication, talk your diet
through with a doctor first. Made for people aged 18 and over.
```

### A képernyőképekről

Ezt nem lehet előre legyártani: valódi appból, valódi adatokkal kell. Amit érdemes
megmutatni, ebben a sorrendben (a Play az első kettőt mutatja a listában):

1. **A mai nap** — a kalóriagyűrű kitöltve, pár naplózott étkezéssel. Ez adja el az
   appot: egy pillantás, és látszik, hol tartasz.
2. **Egy kész étrend** — nyitott nappal, hogy látszódjanak a valódi fogásnevek.
3. **A bevásárlólista** — polcok szerint, néhány kipipálva.
4. **Egy fogás részletei** — tápértékkel és hozzávalókkal.
5. **A beszélgetés** — egy valódi kéréssel és a megerősítő gombbal.
6. **Az allergiaválasztó** — ez sok embernél ez dönt.

Gyakorlati tanácsok:
- Olyan telefonon csináld, aminek a képaránya 16:9 vagy 9:19,5 — a Play mindkettőt
  elfogadja, de a kevert arány csúnyán néz ki egymás mellett.
- Sötét téma vagy világos, de **ne keverd** a képek között.
- A profilban ne legyen valódi neved, ha nem akarod kitenni.
- Az angol listához angol nyelvű képek kellenek — a Play nem fordítja le a képeket.
  Kapcsold át az appot angolra, és csináld meg ugyanazt a hat képet.
